Sparse‑View Neural Reconstruction for Dynamic Rigid‑Fluid Mixed Scenes with Physical Priors
First Author | XX Research Group | XX University

Abstract

Aiming at geometry and distribution distortion problems in sparse‑view reconstruction of dynamic rigid‑fluid mixed scenes, this paper proposes a 3D reconstruction method incorporating physical priors. Conventional neural radiance fields with unified deformation modeling cannot adapt to the topological invariance of rigid bodies and diverse non‑rigid characteristics of fluids, which frequently causes rigid‑body drift, fluid distortion, and failure to distinguish fluid physical properties. In our method, a material‑aware multi‑branch neural field is constructed to separately model rigid bodies and fluids. Temporal feature aggregation and multi‑frame constraints are adopted to correct rigid‑body deformation artifacts. Physical regularization constraints for incompressible liquids and granular flows are introduced respectively. Experiments demonstrate that our approach enables high‑precision reconstruction of multi‑medium interaction scenes and outperforms existing state‑of‑the‑art algorithms.
